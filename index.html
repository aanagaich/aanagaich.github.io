Major Academic & Consultancy Projects

Review of Preliminary Design of the “Statue of Oneness Project”, Omkareshwar, Madhya Pradesh [2022-23]

Served as a Co-Principal Investigator in the Architecture & Planning Team for a prestigious consultancy project worth ₹25 Lakhs, sanctioned by the Madhya Pradesh State Tourism Development Corporation (MPSTDC), Government of Madhya Pradesh .

Contributed towards the critical review and evaluation of preliminary architectural and planning proposals associated with the landmark “Statue of Oneness” development project at Omkareshwar.

The project involved interdisciplinary academic engagement in tourism infrastructure planning, heritage-sensitive development, spatial integration, architectural assessment, and regional-scale urban design considerations.

Study of Design Principles and Indigenous Construction Techniques used in Mahakaal Temple, Ujjain [2023-25]

Worked as a Co-Principal Investigator in a research project worth ₹8.68 Lakhs, funded by the Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) Division, Ministry of Education, Government of India .

The project focused on the academic documentation and analysis of traditional architectural knowledge systems, indigenous construction methodologies, spatial planning principles, and culturally rooted engineering practices embedded within the historic Mahakaal Temple precinct.

The research contributes toward preserving and interpreting traditional Indian architectural wisdom while strengthening interdisciplinary scholarship in heritage conservation, temple architecture, and indigenous construction technologies.
